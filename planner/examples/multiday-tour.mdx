---
title: Multi-Day Tour
description: Hamburg to Munich, 777 km, overnight stop near Ingolstadt.
---

Tours that exceed one legal driving day are planned as multi-day tours automatically — you do
not set anything in the request. The optimizer respects the driver's limits (9 hours of daily
driving, 45 minutes of rest after 4.5 hours) and inserts an **11-hour overnight rest** where the
driving day ends. Whenever possible it places the overnight stop at a charging location, so the
truck starts the next day with a full battery at no extra time cost.

This example plans Hamburg → Munich (777 km): a full driving day, an overnight stop near
Ingolstadt, and a short final leg the next morning. The request is identical to a
[single-day request](/planner/examples/intermediate-stops) — only the route length differs.

## What the optimizer did

Day 1 covers ~690 km with short charging stops placed where charging is fastest (low SoC, high
power). The driving day ends near Ingolstadt at an EnBW charging location: the truck arrives at
18% battery in the evening, rests overnight, and leaves at 90% the next morning. The final
85 km to Munich are driven on day 2, arriving at 08:10 with 72% battery.

You can recognize the overnight stop by its duration: `charge_time_min` and `service_time_min`
cover the **full ~11-hour overnight stay**, not only the effective plugged-in charging time
(see [Limitations](/planner/limitations)). A dedicated overnight-stop field is planned.

<Note>
Time windows (`opening_time`) are not considered on multi-day tours — see
[Time Windows](/planner/examples/time-windows).
</Note>
