## General

Each request optimizes charging for a single route. The road-level route is calculated by HERE — the API itself does not generate or compare alternative routes. Battery levels must be between 0.1 and 1.0 at departure and 0.1 and 0.8 at arrival. Charging stations are searched within a corridor of 1–20 km around the route (default 8 km).

## Rate Limits

Each endpoint allows **120 requests per minute** per client IP. Requests exceeding this limit receive a `429 Too Many Requests` response.

## Vehicles

Only pre-configured vehicles are supported. You cannot submit custom vehicle specifications — use the [Vehicle ID lookup](/planner/vehicles) to find your vehicle. The vehicle database is managed by FRYTE.
